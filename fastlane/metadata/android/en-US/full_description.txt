Rubiyomi is a reader for Japanese books in EPUB.

Add your EPUBs and start reading. Tap any word to see its reading and meanings: the dictionary (JMdict, English) is on your phone, so it works offline, even on a plane.

• Furigana from the book, shown, hidden, or faint until you look a word up
• Vertical or horizontal text, seven page themes, text size, spacing and margins
• Words coloured by your cards on jpdb.io or Anki (AnkiDroid on the phone), so you see at a glance what you already know
• Grade, add or suspend a card right from the word card, with the sentence it came from
• Every word you looked up, searchable, with its sentence and a jump back into the book
• Reading time, characters read and a daily streak

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your books and notes stay on your device; Rubiyomi talks to jpdb.io or Anki only if you set them up.
